Errors, ambiguities and contradictions in the plans **as drawn**, with what I settled on instead.
Found by me or reported by other builders, and credited where I know the source.

Not the same thing as [Plan modifications](plan-modifications.md) — those are deliberate
departures from a correct drawing. These are places where the drawing itself is wrong.
