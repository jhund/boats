# Building a Welsford Long Steps

A build log for a Long Steps 19.5' plywood-and-epoxy sailboat, built from plans by John Welsford.

The log below is chronological and written so that someone building the same boat could follow
along: what I did, in what order, what it cost, what I got wrong, and what I'd do differently.

## About this log

- Written for the next builder, not for me. Everything obvious in hindsight gets written down anyway.
- Amateur build. I am not a boat builder or a naval architect. Follow your plans and your designer, not me.
- Conventions for entries, photos and links: **[CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md)**.

Some links to materials and tools are Amazon affiliate links. If you buy through them I get a
small commission at no extra cost to you. It does not change what I recommend.
