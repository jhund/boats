[← Research index](README.md)

- ## Safety and Security
  - Ability to lock the boat
    - With a bike lock when I have to leave the boat unsupervised somewhere.
    - All I'd need is an opening in the boat that I can feed a lock or a chain through.
      - Grip handles in transition from cuddy to coaming
      - Side drain holes in front of cuddy or at aft of cockpit coaming
      - Through mizzen mast opening and access hatch
      - Through mizzen boomkin tunnel
      - Through holes in each cockpit bulkhead (behind back rest)
  - PFD
    - JW recommends [Pilot Flytväst - Baltic](https://baltic.se/en/produkt/pilot-flytvast/#!)
      - With a tether to the boat
      - To buy it in Canada: https://ca.binnacle.com/m363/Baltic-PFD's/p18327/Baltic-5513-Pilot-with-Harness/product_info.html
  - Radio
    - Phil Built It recommends ICom radios
      - [IC-M94D | Products | Icom America.](https://www.icomamerica.com/lineup/products/IC-M94D/?open=3)
  - Side re-entry support
    - A loop that I can use as a step to help me climb aboard after a capsize over the side of the boat
      - Howard Rice: [Capsize Safety Discussion - West Coast Trailer Sailing Squadron Safety and Capsize Clinic - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TAgxK3h8wMY)
    - Ilur Tilly has an interesting [system of retractable lines](https://youtu.be/DZ0SzoV31ng?si=zKb00BoW5J4XBtOY&t=586) to step on for re-entry
  - Tether: Make it easy to attach myself to the boat with a tether.
