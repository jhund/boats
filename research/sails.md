# Sails

[← Research index](README.md)

  - Buy the sails before building the masts and yards so that I can make sure everything fits, e.g., that the mast and yard are tall enough to raise the sail well above the cockpit for good headroom. Also helps with putting the rigging hardware in the right places (cleats, blocks, etc.). Basically figure out the rigging early on to inform certain design decisions.
  - JW says that the main sail could be replaced by the "OZ Goose" sail from Really Simple Sails (RSS)
    - If doing so, the boom needs to be 200mm longer and the yard 600mm longer.
    - RSS can also make the mizzen
  - JW and Mark Baker both bought their LS sails from RSS
  - Michael Storer's website has great info on Oz Goose sails
  - This video by boat rambler shows a very simple reefing system: [Reefing a Lug Sail - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=beh1CnDPljI)
