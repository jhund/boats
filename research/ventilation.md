# Ventilation

[← Research index](README.md)

  - All enclosed volumes of air must be ventilated to allow for pressure adjustment. From the Sailing moga forum:
    - The ability of sealed compartments to breathe when you change elevation needs to be considered. For example, I launched on a 4,400' (1,300 m) elevation lake and the daggerboard became an interference fit. The boat was built and lives near sea level and the board and/or case had plumped up at altitude. I suspect at higher altitudes sealed compartments could stress the structure itself. The tiniest of holes will address this.
