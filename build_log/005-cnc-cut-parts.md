## Notes & gotchas

- Things the plans don't tell you.
- Measurements that mattered more than expected.

- ### Plans errata
  - Joel Bergen sent me this:
    - Sheet 3, Bulkhead #1
      - Location of top of crown
         675 dimension? or is it 760 - 75 = 685?
      - 675 is too low.
         685 is just a little high.
      - 681 seems right when fitting curve through rest of points
    - Bulkhead #2
      - Sheet 3, Bulkhead 2, Doubler - item number 10 says it is 118 mm wide, aft face
        Sheet
        8, Main Mast Box view. inside of box width is 106. Sides (item 1) are
        9mm. 106+9+9=124. Does not match 118 width of B-2 doubler.
        Suspect B-2 doubler should be 124mm wide instead of 118mm wide. Or maybe Mast Box sides (item 1) should be 6mm instead of 9mm.
  - Mark Baker said that Plank #1 does not work as drawn
    - Need to add a bit more material aft where the curve goes in the other direction
  - Tiller tunnel cutout in transom is too wide in the plans. I had to insert filler pieces to make it narrower.
- ### CNC cut files
  - The jigsaw puzzle joints are all in similar places on adjacent planks. Consider shifting some of them so there are no two weak seams down the side of the hull on either side.
  - Model the boat in 3D in OnShape.
    - Use their "flatten surface" feature to get the plank shapes. At least confirm that they are similar to the ones provided by Joel's CAD files.
  - I ended up using Joel Bergen's DXF files and made a few modifications (See the order for islandlaser.ca)
