- ## About
  - An evolution and combination of the best features of Walkabout and SCAMP:
    - Walkabout's fast hull
    - SCAMP's features like dry floor, cuddy, off-center board, etc.
  - A cat yawl, that’s a big main sail well forward and a mizzen down aft, that leaves the centre of the boat clear for the rower.
  - Specs:
    - LOA 19'6" (5.95m)
    - Beam 5'7" (67", 170cm)
    - Weight: 485 lbs (220kg)
    - Similar rigging to Walkabout: Balanced lug and mizzen
- ## My motivation
  - I'm hoping that building and using this boat will make sure that there is enough adventure in my life.
  - I want to be able to go on voyages to places where I can escape the business and find peace and quiet. Ideally with a good companion.
- ## Compared to Walkabout
  - LS Pros
    - Can sleep 2
    - More seaworthy boat
    - Cuddy for protection and comfort. Better camping experience
    - More stable (water ballast)
    - CNC cut files for more parts available, esp. the planks!
    - Dry/self draining floor
  - LS Cons
    - Heavier
    - More difficult to build (is it? I can get CNC cut files for all key parts)
    - More windage when rowing
